Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DigyBiz (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects the personal information you share when you use this website or contact us.

1. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, phone number and email address when you submit our “Free Proposal” form.
  • Technical details: the page you submitted the form from and basic information your browser shares automatically.
  • Messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp, email or phone.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare your proposal.
  • To contact you about the services you asked about.
  • To keep internal records of enquiries and improve our services.

We will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

3. Where your information is stored

Form submissions are stored securely in a private Google Sheet managed by DigyBiz using Google services. Access is limited to people who need it to respond to you.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with trusted service providers that help us run our business (for example Google Workspace or WhatsApp), or when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you and manage our business relationship, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop receiving messages from us. Contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies by itself. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If we add analytics or advertising tools (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel) in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.
