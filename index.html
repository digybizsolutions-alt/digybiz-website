FAQ

Questions business owners often ask

How soon can we get started?

After a short discovery call, we usually share your proposal within a few days. Most social media and ad campaigns can start within one to two weeks of approval; websites typically take two to four weeks depending on size.

How much ad budget do I need for Meta Ads?

It depends on your goals, location and industry. We’ll recommend a starting budget in your proposal and begin with small tests, then increase spend only on what performs. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta and is separate from our service fee.

What exactly do you automate?

Common automations include saving form and WhatsApp enquiries to a sheet or CRM, sending instant acknowledgement messages, notifying you of new leads, follow-up reminders and simple lead tracking. We start simple and add more as your process grows.

Will I get reports on performance?

Yes. You get clear, regular reports on reach, leads, cost per lead and the actions we’re taking next — in plain language.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. We offer one-time projects (like websites and videos) and flexible monthly plans for ongoing marketing. Terms are agreed up front in your proposal.

Can you work with my existing website or social pages?

Absolutely. We can improve what you already have, connect it to automation, or rebuild where it makes sense.
